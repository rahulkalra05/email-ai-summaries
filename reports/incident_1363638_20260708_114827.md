## Common Patterns Identified
* **Platform:** Compro Builder (ComproDLS) on Cambridge One.
* **Symptom:** The ingestion, publishing, or promotion pipeline stalls indefinitely ("stuck in processing" or "stuck ingesting") at a specific lifecycle stage (QA, Staging, or Production).
* **Technical Pattern:** The pipeline hang is typically triggered by database status flags failing to update, metadata mismatches, invalid umbrella product states, or corruption in specific learning path modules during import or cloning.

## Root Cause Analysis

* **Confirmed causes:** 
  * The ingestion/publishing process for component `ttrscbpftrsb` is stalled in the Production environment of the Builder tool.
* **Suspected causes:**
  * **Module/Learning Path Corruption:** Similar to **Ticket 1348542** and **Ticket 1351137**, a specific module or learning object within the "Test & Train Mini C2 Teacher's Resources" learning path may be corrupted, preventing the pipeline from completing.
  * **Database Status Flag Mismatch:** Similar to **Ticket 1112505**, the underlying content may have ingested successfully, but the status flag failed to transition from "ingesting" to "complete."
  * **Unstable Stack/Event Handling:** Similar to **Ticket 1064434**, an underlying infrastructure disruption (e.g., EventBridge Stack) may have left the umbrella product in an invalid state.
* **Missing evidence:**

  * Diagnostics on whether specific learning path modules or large assets within the component are failing validation.
