# Incident Investigation Report: 1359073

## Reported Issue Summary
A user is experiencing connection issues when attempting to launch assignments via the Blackboard application on the Cambridge One (C1) platform. While the platform support team cannot replicate the issue and notes that some activities were successfully completed, the user is currently unable to proceed with further assignments.

## Affected Systems
*   Cambridge One (C1) Platform
*   Blackboard (LTI integration)
*   Metrica (Assessment Engine)

## Common Patterns
*   **Platform:** All incidents involve the Cambridge One LTI integration with Blackboard and the Metrica assessment engine.
*   **Symptom:** Recurring redirect loops or failure to launch assignments/tests.
*   **Synchronization:** Frequent state mismatches where the student's progress is marked as "In Progress" or "Completed" in Metrica, while the Cambridge One platform fails to reflect this, resulting in launch blockage.

## Root Cause Analysis
- **Confirmed causes:** None. Internal logs indicate no errors, and the user has previously completed work, suggesting an intermittent synchronization or state-capture issue rather than a platform-wide outage.
- **Suspected causes:** A state synchronization mismatch between Cambridge One and Metrica (Tickets 1083629, 1058626), or a failure in the score payload transmission from Metrica (Tickets 1044716, 1013019).
- **Missing evidence:** Specific student logs and Metrica-side attempt status (current "In progress" vs "Completed" status) are required to confirm a synchronization error.

## Recommended Next Steps
1.  **Request Metrica Status:** Contact the Metrica team to verify the status of the affected student's attempts and confirm if a valid score payload exists (Reference: Ticket 1100066, 1044716).
2.  **Audit Launch Metadata:** Verify if the launch action is correctly set to "prepopulate" rather than "review" or an erroneous state (Reference: Ticket 1083629).
3.  **Validate Attempt State:** Review the User Activity (UA) state to determine if multiple launches triggered an inconsistent "submitCount" (Reference: Ticket 1013019).

## Recommended Resolution
The most likely resolution is a manual push of the score payload from Metrica to Cambridge One or a reset of the specific test attempt by the Metrica team to clear the synchronization mismatch. 
**Confidence Level:** High (based on the high frequency of similar state synchronization failures between C1 and Metrica in historical data).
